Human-in-the-Loop Workflow Assistant

Preparing structured proposals while people retain final decisions.

Background

A business team needed help preparing consistent records from routine messages.

Problem

Repetitive data preparation required careful handling of missing information and ambiguous requests.

My contribution

I developed a workflow assistant, prepared documentation, and gathered stakeholder feedback during my summer internship.

Requirements

  • Extract only supported information.
  • Ask for clarification when details are missing.
  • Prepare a readable proposal.
  • Keep final decisions with a person.

Approach

Understand the request, check required context, prepare a proposal, and route it for human review.

Responsible handling

The assistant supported data preparation without making autonomous decisions about people. No employee records, internal policies, or access configuration are included here.

Delivery

  1. Gathered requirements.
  2. Defined validation and clarification behavior.
  3. Prepared example workflows.
  4. Documented maintenance considerations.

Validation approach

Reviewed normal, incomplete, and ambiguous examples, checking that uncertain input led to clarification rather than invented details.

Outcome

Delivered the assistant and handoff documentation. Adoption and accuracy feedback were still being gathered at handoff.

Lessons learned

Clear limits, human review, and maintainable documentation matter as much as automation.
